Tones

In a tone language the pitch contour of a syllable is part of the word. Five levels are enough to write it: 5 is high, 1 is low, 3 is your own pitch. Draw a contour on the pad, or use the four Mandarin tones, and hear it on a vowel.

Press a row, slide up or down, and let go where the tone ends.

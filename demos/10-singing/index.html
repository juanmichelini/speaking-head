Singing

Singing is speech where someone else chose the pitch and the length of every syllable. Each syllable gets a note: its pitch (C4 is middle C, A4 is 440 Hz) and its beats, like C4:2. Lesson 7 fits the syllable into the time, and lesson 3's pitch line carries the note.
